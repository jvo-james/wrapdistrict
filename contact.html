FAQs

Good to know.

Quick answers before you place an order or get in touch.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available for a fee. The exact fee and timing depend on your location and are confirmed with your order.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes. Pickup is available from Tema Community 25, Devtraco once your order is ready.

Can I customise my food?

Yes. Selected menu items let you choose size, protein, sauces and extras in the order builder.

What are your order hours?

Orders are available Monday to Friday from 5pm. On Saturday and Sunday, orders are available from 9am.

How can I support Feed the Street?

You can contribute through the Feed the Street page or contact us about logistics, partnerships and other forms of support.
