Why we keep doing it

A meal can feel like somebody thought about you.

Feed the Street started with a simple idea: take the care that goes into a Wrap District meal and let it reach beyond the shop.

We cook, pack and share with the same attention we'd want for ourselves. And every outreach gives us another reason to do it better the next time.
