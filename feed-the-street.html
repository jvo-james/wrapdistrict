Where it started

A meal can carry care.

Feed the Street is how Wrap District extends the same thought and effort behind every order into the wider community. We cook, pack and share meals with children while building each outreach to be more organised and more impactful than the last.

The journey

Growing with purpose.

Accra Mall and Madina

We fed 100 children around Accra Mall and in Madina. It was an important first milestone and a reminder that a simple meal can create a meaningful moment.
