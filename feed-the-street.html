First outreach · May 2026

A first step in Accra.

100children reached

Our first Feed the Street outreach reached 100 children in Accra. It gave the project its starting point and showed us what can happen when people choose to show up for one another.

Second outreach · October 2026

More than 800.
One school day.

800+children reached

In October, we visited a basic school and shared with more than 800 children. The day was a bigger step for Feed the Street and a reminder that small acts of care can reach further than we expect.
